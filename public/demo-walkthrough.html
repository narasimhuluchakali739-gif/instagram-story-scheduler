Narration & Walkthrough

Step 1: Welcome to the Instagram Story Scheduler prototype. Notice the clean Instagram Dark Theme and the top Story Tray.

📑 Step-by-Step Feature Breakdown

1 Open "Your Story" Circle 0:00 - 0:05

Top stories tray features live gradient rings. Tap "Your Story" with the blue '+' badge to open the camera creator.

2 Story Editor & "Schedule Story" Button 0:06 - 0:12

Add photos, text, stickers, or filters. The new glowing pink/purple Schedule Story button is front-and-center.

3 Pick Exact Date & Time (Or 12:00 AM) 0:13 - 0:19

Choose any date and time, toggle Close Friends privacy, or tap one-click 12:00 AM Midnight birthday drops.

4 Confirmation & Celebration Confetti 0:20 - 0:24

Success screen confirms target date and time with animated checkmark and celebratory confetti.

5 Scheduled Dashboard & Actions 0:25 - 0:27

View upcoming queues with live countdowns. Reschedule, cancel, or tap "Drop Now" for instant simulation!

6 Story Auto-Publishes Live 0:28 - 0:30

When target time strikes, story auto-promotes to Published and opens in the Instagram Story Player with progress bars.
